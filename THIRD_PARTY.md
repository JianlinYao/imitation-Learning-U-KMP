# Research sources and retained material

The existing `2Dletters/` data and `Imitationlearning-KMP/` notebooks were already present in this repository before the reproducibility completion. The dissertation identifies Yanlong Huang's Robot Inference Library as the reference source for the demonstration data and KMP methods:

https://github.com/yanlongtu/robInfLib-matlab

The new package implements the published mathematical methods in Python and preserves the original research files for comparison. It does not claim authorship of DMP, ProMP, GP, KMP or the uncertainty-aware KMP method.

Research references:

- Ijspeert et al. (2013), *Dynamical Movement Primitives: Learning Attractor Models for Motor Behaviors*, Neural Computation 25(2), 328–373.
- Paraschos et al. (2013), *Probabilistic Movement Primitives*, NeurIPS 26.
- Rasmussen and Williams (2006), *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning*. https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/
- Huang et al. (2019), *Kernelized Movement Primitives*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08638
- Silvério et al. (2019), *Uncertainty-Aware Imitation Learning using Kernelized Movement Primitives*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.02114
